Research

My research is in commutative algebra and algebraic combinatorics. On the commutative algebra side, my interests include (but are not limited to) questions about depth, multiplicities and measures of singularity, Cohen-Macaulay representation theory, and  derived categories. I have been working recently on trace ideals/submodules, Gorenstein properties of DG algebras, and homological rigidity conjectures such as the Auslander-Reiten conjecture, in particular exploiting techniques from noncommutative algebra. In algebraic combinatorics, I am interested in Stanley-Reisner theory and understanding certain homological conditions, such as bounds on depth or Serre conditions, from the simplicial complex perspective. I have been working recently on studying monomial ideals that are derived via certain operations from matroid complexes and well as a notion of higher crosscut complexes.
